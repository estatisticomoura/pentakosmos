[font_size=26][color=#f0c96a][b]How Pentakosmos was born[/b][/color][/font_size]

It all started at a table, among friends, with a card game I loved and rules we kept changing. How many tokens with three players? What if the philosophers asked for a little less? I wanted real answers, not guesses.

[font_size=22][color=#f0c96a]Thousands of games before yours[/color][/font_size]
With Claude, an artificial-intelligence assistant, I turned every idea into an experiment. My computer played tens of thousands of games, and the numbers had the final word. Every rule of the Pentakosmos mode survived that test — including the ones that went against my intuition.

[font_size=22][color=#f0c96a]A balanced cosmos[/color][/font_size]
The deck was born from 18 formulas I designed: each one creates the same card for all five elements, so no element is favoured. In the Timaeus, Plato links the elements to the five regular solids — and that is where the game found its soul. The tokens became slowly turning solids, the academy's visitors became philosophers, and the name came by itself: [b]Pentakosmos[/b], the cosmos of the five.

[font_size=22][color=#f0c96a]Opponents that learned to play[/color][/font_size]
Lysis, Crito, Phaedrus, Theaetetus and Timaeus do not follow scripts: a genetic algorithm tuned their strategy over hundreds of thousands of games, and a tournament decided who deserved each place.

[font_size=22][color=#f0c96a]Made with care, without ads[/color][/font_size]
Classical music performed anew for the game, 38 languages, statistics for every game, rules you can combine as you like. The base game is free, ad-free and open source — and it will stay that way.

[font_size=22][color=#f0c96a]The expansion[/color][/font_size]
If Pentakosmos won you over, the expansion takes the academy further: 17 governors of Ancient Greece — Pericles, Cleopatra, Draco, Gorgo of Sparta… — who change the way their holder wins, all 10 writers, and the freedom to create your own decks, philosophers, writers and governors in a spreadsheet and share them with friends. It is also the best way to tell the game: keep going.
