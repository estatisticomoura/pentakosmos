[font_size=26][color=#f0c96a][b]Archaic[/b][/color][/font_size]
The Archaic mode keeps the traditional rhythm of this kind of card game and adds the writers to the academy. It is played with the Archaic deck. Turns, buying, reserving and the end of the game work as in Pentakosmos; these are the differences:

• [b]Element tokens per pile:[/b] 4 / 5 / 7 / 8 for 2 / 3 / 4 / 5 players.
• [b]Spheres:[/b] always 5.
• [b]Take 2 of the same element:[/b] only from a pile with at least 4 tokens.
• [b]Market:[/b] 4 cards per group, also with 5 players.
• [b]Philosophers:[/b] the requirement must be met exactly, and each philosopher is worth 3 points.
• [b]Writers:[/b] 2 writers are on the table as well (the base game brings Homer and Sophocles; the expansion, all 10). Each one asks for cards in the element combinations the philosophers do not use (3 + 3 of two elements, or 2 + 2 + 2 of three), must be met exactly and is worth 1 point. In the same turn you can receive one philosopher and one writer.
• [b]No specialization victory:[/b] only the 15-point goal ends the game.

[font_size=22][color=#f0c96a]The Archaic deck[/color][/font_size]
Like the Pentakosmos deck, its 90 cards come from 18 formulas applied to each of the five elements, with the same average costs per group, but with its own molds: the costs fall on other positions of the cycle air → water → aether → fire → earth.

Taking 1 or 2 tokens of different elements is allowed in every mode, so a nearly full hand never forces you to take tokens only to return them.

The game was designed for 2 to 4 players; 5 players is an extension.
