STYLE GUIDE (applies to every image of this set):
Cinematic digital painting set in Ancient Greece (roughly 600-150 BC), as if it were a frame of concept art for a high-budget historical film. Painterly realism with visible but refined brushwork, epic sense of scale, atmospheric depth, volumetric light (god rays, haze, dust or sea spray in the air), golden-hour or dramatic natural lighting, deep but readable shadows. Rich, harmonious, slightly desaturated palette, warm marble and bronze tones, with one dominant accent colour given in the scene description.
Historically plausible architecture, clothing, armour, ships and objects (chitons, himations, peplos, Corinthian and Attic helmets, Doric and Ionic columns, painted marble, bronze, terracotta, triremes). Statues and temples may show traces of their original paint.
People are dignified, expressive and beautiful in a natural, non-glamour way, with realistic Mediterranean features and varied ages.
Composition: vertical, made to sit inside a playing card. Keep the main subject in the upper two thirds and leave the lower third calmer and less detailed. No hard frame, no border, no vignette shaped like a card.
Strictly NO text, letters, inscriptions, numbers, logos, signatures or watermarks anywhere in the image. No modern objects. No gore. No nudity.
